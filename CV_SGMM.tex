\begin{fullwidth}
\noindent I study how demographic history---migration, admixture, and drift---has shaped genetic
variation in human and non-human populations. My doctoral work combines whole-genome sequencing with
coalescent-based demographic inference to reconstruct the pre-Columbian and post-contact history of
Mexican populations. Earlier work examined how codon composition shapes mRNA stability and translation.
I am looking for a postdoctoral position applying statistical and population genomic methods to
questions in evolutionary demography, ancient DNA, or comparative genomics.
\end{fullwidth}
